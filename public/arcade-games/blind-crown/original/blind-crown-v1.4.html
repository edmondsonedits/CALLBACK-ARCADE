Hide. Predict. Survive.

1 · Pick a secret position

Tap in Place mode, use WASD/arrow keys, or use the touch stick. Cover blocks shots. Rivals cannot see your position during planning.

2 · Predict and commit

Select Aim and point your crossbow. Q/E turns continuously; −1°/+1° makes precise adjustments. In first/third-person, drag to turn and tap the small private map to relocate.

3 · Reveal and volley

Lock when ready; unlock to change your plan before time expires. At zero, everybody freezes and fires—even archers hit by another arrow. Nobody can react during the reveal.

Win the tournament

The arena tightens between rounds. Last survivor wins. At the round limit, points decide; a total wipe also uses points. Equal points retain the existing seat-order tiebreak.

Privacy and room play

Keep your own screen private while planning. Only use the public TV spectator link on a shared display. Room outcomes come from the server; reconnects recover the same seat for up to two minutes.

The downloadable HTML supports solo + bots. Multiplayer needs the included Node server; it is not hosted by this download.
