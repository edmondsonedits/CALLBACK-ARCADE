Hide. Predict. Survive.

1 · Pick a secret position

Tap in Place mode, use WASD/arrow keys, or use the touch stick. Stone absorbs; striped Orchard hedges block movement but arrows pass; Frost ice reflects once; Clockwork shutters announce their open lanes each round. Rivals cannot see your position during planning.

2 · Predict and commit

Select Aim and point your crossbow. Q/E turns continuously; −1°/+1° makes precise adjustments. In first/third-person, drag to turn and tap the small private map to relocate.

3 · Reveal and volley

Lock when ready; unlock to change your plan before time expires. At zero, everybody freezes and fires—even archers hit by another arrow. Nobody can react during the reveal.

Win the tournament

The arena tightens between rounds. Last survivor wins. At the round limit, points decide; a total wipe also uses points. Equal points retain the existing seat-order tiebreak.

Learn from a volley

After a round, Review volleys pauses your local match. Pick any completed round and scrub the arrows to see who hit whom, what cover stopped and which lanes stayed open. Previous rounds do not predict new hidden positions. Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims, A locks, X/Y change mode, Start pauses. Release the sticks and buttons after opening a menu.

Privacy and room play

Keep your own screen private while planning. Only use the public TV spectator link on a shared display. Room outcomes come from the server; reconnects recover the same seat for up to two minutes.

This standalone supports one human plus bots. Multiplayer requires a separately hosted Node server from the full source package; no room server is included or hosted by this HTML.
