# Callback quality corpus

Representative graph:

- Anchor: The Gum Reaper — winner of “Invent the mascot for a dentist office that has already been investigated twice.”
- Voice: “Brush twice. Or meet me.”
- World: “All elevators require eye contact.”
- Consequence: “The elevator opens directly into HR.”

## Reject

- “The Gum Reaper is in an elevator. What happens?” — merely places two nouns together; no causal escalation.
- “The Gum Reaper, a moon lawyer, and five unrelated ducks open a hotel. Name it.” — contaminates established lore with unrelated concepts.
- “Does The Gum Reaper like elevators?” — closed-ended and offers almost no comic room.
- “Write something funny about all the earlier jokes.” — vague, high writing burden, and not a situation.
- “Who wins the elevator contest?” — invents a mechanic and asks the model to imply an outcome.

## Accept

- “After campaigning on ‘Brush twice. Or meet me,’ The Gum Reaper is put in charge of enforcing mandatory elevator eye contact. What goes wrong on day one?” — preserves all relationships, escalates causally, and ends with a concrete open question.
- “The Gum Reaper's toothpaste commercial must now obey the new elevator eye-contact law. What's the warning at the end of the ad?” — coherent, answerable, and gives weaker writers a useful comedic machine.

## Strong Remix

“Following the incident where the elevator opened directly into HR, The Gum Reaper refuses to resign. His only statement remains ‘Brush twice. Or meet me,’ and mandatory elevator eye contact is still official policy. He has now been put in charge of the country. What's his first message to the nation?”

This passes because it preserves the established causal story, escalates only once, uses four compatible memories, and finishes with one short answerable question.

## Evaluation rubric

Score each candidate from 0–1:

- Coherence: Can a player explain why every recalled element is present?
- Meaning preservation: Are the room's actual jokes still recognizable?
- Causality: Does one established element create pressure on another?
- Answerability: Can a player understand the task and start writing within three seconds?
- Surprise: Does the combination create a fresh comic turn without random noise?
- Economy: Is the setup brief enough for a TV and the final question singular?

A shippable callback needs coherence ≥ 0.74, answerability ≥ 0.70, surprise ≥ 0.42, safe content, semantic connection, and no closed/repetitive flags. Remix coherence must be ≥ 0.78.
