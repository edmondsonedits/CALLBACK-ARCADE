THE DEFENDER'S HANDBOOK

Catch. Aim. Counter.

  • Guard your gate: move left / right along your colored sector. The numbered pennant identifies each defender. Orange landing marks warn of incoming shots.
  • Catch and release: hold Magnet to catch up to 4 balls. Move while holding to aim the dashed volley guide, then release to fire. Release before the heat bar fills.
  • Counter: tap Pulse just before contact. A centered hit in the gold timing window gives a Perfect counter and a faster recharge. Magnet and Pulse cannot overlap.
  • Win: be the last gate standing. Sealed gates become walls. Defense Drill runs for 60 seconds with no eliminations and tracks catches, counters and misses.
