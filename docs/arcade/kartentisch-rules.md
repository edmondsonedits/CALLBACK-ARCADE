# Kartentisch rules

Kartentisch is an original King Prompter trick-taking game for three to eight controllers. It uses a seeded shuffle of a standard 52-card deck and deals five cards to each player. The next undealt card sets the visible trump suit.

The opening leader is the first seated player. Players take turns clockwise, each committing one card face down. After the leader commits, the led suit is visible; a player who holds that suit must follow it. Committed cards remain hidden until the host reveals the trick.

The highest trump wins the trick. If no trump was played, the highest card in the led suit wins. The winner scores one trick and leads the next trick. After five tricks, most tricks wins; tied scores produce co-winners. If the deadline expires, the room automatically completes the trick by choosing each missing player's lowest legal card in turn order. A final result reveals the dealt hands. Private hands, current hidden plays, and the shuffle seed are never sent to host or spectator snapshots beforehand.
