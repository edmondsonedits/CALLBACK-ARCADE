# The 100-prompt Sketch & Sorcery catalogue

Researched 2026-10-30. This replaces the previous sixteen-example pool.

## What the evidence supports

This is a curated selection, not a statistically ranked top 100. No reliable public prompt-by-prompt leaderboard was found. Official examples, positive hands-on reviews, first-person player reports and published drawing-game lists support the selection. A source's game rating, page views or video likes do not establish ratings for individual prompts.

Twenty-one entries use published phrases with capitalization or leading articles normalized. The remaining 79 are shortened or reframed adaptations of published ideas, including Gartic Phone suggestions suited to this game's shared-prompt still drawings. Community-reported captions are not claimed to be verified official Jackbox prompts. The numbers below identify entries; they do not rank popularity.

Selection rules: a short drawable visual hook; recognizable subjects; an odd juxtaposition, literal interpretation or comic predicament; varied joke structures; no dependence on private group lore; at most 45 characters. This is editorial judgment informed by the sources, not evidence that every entry will suit every group.

## Source notes

- **review** — [Mr. Panda — hands-on Drawful 2 review](https://mrpanda2002.wordpress.com/2018/06/21/drawful-2-review/). Reviewer quotes memorable concrete and abstract Drawful examples; no individual prompt ratings.
- **drawful** — [Jackbox — Drawful](https://www.jackboxgames.com/games/drawful). Official example; not a popularity ranking.
- **director** — [Jackbox — Q&A with Drawful: Animate director Spencer Ham](https://www.jackboxgames.com/blog/mini-qa-with-drawful-animate-game-director-spencer-ham). Director describes daily playtests and quotes a prompt; not an individual rating.
- **format** — [Storythings — Formats Unpacked: Drawful](https://formatsunpacked.storythings.com/p/formats-unpacked-drawful). Published format analysis with example prompts; not audience voting data.
- **product** — [Jason Shen — The Product Genius Behind Jackbox Games](https://www.jasonshen.com/the-product-genius-behind-jackbox-games/). Player/product analysis quoting examples; not prompt-level ratings.
- **steam** — [Drawful 2 — official Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/442070/Drawful_2/). Official sample prompts. Overall game reviews do not rate these prompts individually.
- **community** — [Eagle Time — Drawful player screenshot thread](https://eagle-time.org/printthread.php?tid=1725). First-person play reports and favorite-drawing batches. Some remembered captions are uncertain; these are community-reported ideas, not verified official prompt text or ranked favorites.
- **party** — [OnlineParty.Games — Drawful-style party starter](https://onlineparty.games/games/jackbox-style/drawful-2). Author-published drawing suggestions; editorial list, not community ratings.
- **host** — [Lilach Bullock — Funny Gartic Phone Prompts](https://www.lilachbullock.com/funny-gartic-phone-prompts-for-game-night/). Host reports playing team sessions and offers ideas; no individual prompt votes.
- **wikihow** — [wikiHow — Funny, Quirky and Weird Gartic Phone Prompts](https://www.wikihow.com/Gartic-Phone-Prompts). Edited drawing-game suggestion list. Adapted for a shared-prompt still drawing; not Drawful ratings.
- **hugateen** — [HugATeen — Gartic Phone Game: Funny Sentences, Phrases and Prompts](https://hugateen.com/activities/games/gartic-phone-game/). Published drawing-game idea collection. Adaptations are editorial choices, not verified player favorites.

Additional checks: [Jackbox's writing guidance](https://www.jackboxgames.com/blog/how-to-write-a-good-quiplash-prompt) supports specificity, variety and a prompt that immediately suggests possible responses; it is general writing guidance, not a Drawful ranking. [Steam's creator thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/442070/discussions/0/358415206096222692/) confirms the original custom-prompt length constraint but mostly shares episode codes, so it was not treated as individual popularity evidence. Broad Reddit searches mostly returned game rankings or unrelated conversations, which were excluded. [Let's Play's custom episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJ2CUN-zJ-8) explicitly collects that group's earlier favorite answers, but does not establish a general top 100 and depends heavily on group lore; it was not copied into the catalogue.

## In-game behavior

All 100 appear in the phone's scrollable inspiration list. Borrowing one keeps it classified as an example; player-written originals keep their election tie priority. When submissions leave gaps, the server samples examples with the existing private match seed, instead of always using the first five. No ratings, source metadata or category claims are shown as player authorship.
